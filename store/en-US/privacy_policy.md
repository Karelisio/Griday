# Griday privacy policy

*Last updated: [publication date]*

Griday is a daily logic game. This page explains what data is processed when you use it.

## What Griday does not collect

- No account, no Griday server: grids are generated on your phone.
- Your progress (results, streak, statistics, settings) is stored **on your device**, and nowhere else for Griday. If Android backup is turned on, it is included in your personal Google backup (restored on a new device). Uninstalling the app erases it from the device.
- The daily reminder is a local notification, scheduled on your phone, with no server involved.

## Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads provided by **Google AdMob** (videos you choose to watch in exchange for a hint, a streak freeze or an archive day, and a full-screen ad after some games). To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect and process:

- the device advertising ID;
- the IP address (approximate location);
- technical information about the device and system;
- interactions with the ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your consent is requested before any personalized advertising (Google's consent tool). You can review your choices at any time in **Settings › Privacy choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Learn more: [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

With **Premium**, no ads are loaded.

## Purchases

The Premium purchase is processed by **Google Play**. Griday never receives your payment details: the app only reads the purchase confirmation to unlock Premium.

## Children

Griday is not specifically directed at children under 13.
