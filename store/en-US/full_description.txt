A new grid every day. The same one for everyone.

Griday is an elegant, relaxing daily logic game. One grid a day, identical for every player, to solve in a few minutes with your morning coffee… or to savour a little longer at the weekend.

♛ QUEENS
Place one queen in each row, each column and each colored region. Two queens can never touch, not even diagonally. Easy to learn, surprisingly deep.

🧩 100% LOGIC
Every grid has a single solution and can be solved by pure deduction: no guessing, no luck. Each puzzle is checked by a solver before it reaches you.

📈 A WEEK THAT RAMPS UP
Monday is gentle, Sunday is tough. Grids grow from 6×6 to 10×10 through the week, and the difficulty follows the same rhythm.

💡 HINTS THAT EXPLAIN
Stuck? A hint shows you the cell to play and explains why, with the deduction technique behind it. You get better with every game.

♾️ UNLIMITED MODE
Want another grid? Pick a size and a difficulty, and play as much as you like.

🔥 YOUR DAILY ROUTINE
• Daily streak and personal best
• Statistics: wins, average time, time distribution
• Share your result with emojis, without spoilers
• Daily reminder at the time of your choice
• Archive: replay past days from the calendar
• Streak freeze to protect your streak on a day off

🎨 MATERIAL YOU
A modern Android design that picks up your wallpaper colors, in light or dark mode, with smooth animations and polished haptics. Grids designed to be readable and accessible.

✈️ OFFLINE
No account, no connection needed: grids are generated on your phone. Your data stays with you.

🌍 Available in English and French.

Griday is free, with an optional Premium upgrade to play without ads, with unlimited archive access and hints.
